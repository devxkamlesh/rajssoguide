## ❌ Skip these (not a fit / risk)

| Platform | Why skip |
|----------|----------|
| Product Hunt, G2, Capterra, TrustRadius | For SaaS products with reviews — an info guide isn't a fit; listings sit empty. |
| Google Business Profile | Requires a physical location or in-person service area; a website isn't eligible. |
| Wikidata / Wikipedia | Notability not established yet — premature entries get deleted. Revisit after real press/citations. |
| Paid "500-directory submission" gigs | DR-under-10 link farms; can trigger Google spam signals on a YMYL site. |
